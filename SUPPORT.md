# Support

For assistance with the Food Delivery backend:

- **Engineering:** Post in Slack `#food-delivery-backend` or email `backend@bengobox.com`
- **Production incidents:** Page the on-call engineer via PagerDuty (`Food Delivery Backend` service)
- **Security incidents:** Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md)
- **Product/feature questions:** `#food-delivery-product` Slack channel
