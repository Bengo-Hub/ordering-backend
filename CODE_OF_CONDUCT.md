# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the BengoBox Food Delivery backend project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward other people
- Being welcoming and inclusive of diverse backgrounds and viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- Sexualised language, imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behaviour and will take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to [security@bengobox.com](mailto:security@bengobox.com). All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction** – Private communication clarifying expected behaviour
2. **Warning** – Official warning with consequences for continued behaviour
3. **Temporary Ban** – Temporary removal from community spaces
4. **Permanent Ban** – Permanent removal from community spaces
